Featured case study

Jackers

A gamified personal-finance concept that makes everyday money habits engaging and sustainable. The five chapters below trace the reasoning, from the problem to the proposed experience.

Chapter 01 · The problem01

Budgeting tools record spending well, but rarely sustain attention

Most trackers present money as a retrospective list. Few give people a reason to return, so consistency fades within weeks.

Low motivation · fragmented habits
Chapter 02 · The insight02

Lasting change depends on habit design, not on feature count

Habits form through a repeatable loop: a clear cue, a small action, visible progress and a meaningful reward.

Behaviour before features
Chapter 03 · The solution03

Financial routines, reframed as progression

Jackers turns budgeting tasks into missions, personas and milestones, with rewards that reinforce each completed habit.

Identity · challenges · rewards
Chapter 04 · The experience04

A simple, repeatable journey

Users choose a financial persona, set a weekly objective, track their progress and earn Joker Coins when a challenge is completed.

Choose · act · earn
Chapter 05 · The outcome05

Progress that is easy to see

The intended outcomes are clearer spending awareness, more consistent budgeting and stronger motivation to save. These are design goals, not measured results.

Designed outcomes · not measured claims
